Privacy Policy — Atronm Outreach

Last updated 4 October 2026

Atronm Outreach is a private tool that Moiz Ahmad (Atronm Technologies) runs on his own computer to manage his own business outreach. It is not offered to the public, and the only Google account that signs in to it is his own.

What Google data it uses, and why

  • Gmail — to send outreach emails from his account, read replies to those emails, and save reply drafts for him to review.
  • Google Sheets — to read and update one spreadsheet that holds his list of business contacts.
  • Google Calendar — to read his calendar events so that booked meetings are marked in that spreadsheet.

Where the data goes

  • Data is processed on his computer and stored in his own Google account. There is no separate server or database.
  • To draft a message, the business's public details (name, category, city, website findings) or the text of a reply are sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which returns the draft text.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Business contacts

Contacts come from public sources such as OpenStreetMap and businesses' own websites. Every email includes a way to opt out. Anyone who replies "unsubscribe", or writes to the address below, is removed and not contacted again.

Atronm Outreach's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
