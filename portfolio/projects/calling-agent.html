AI Calling Agent Simulator

Dial the agent and play the person who picks up. It introduces itself as an automated assistant, follows a call script, deals with “who is this?”, “call me later” and “stop calling me”, books the outcome and logs the call. No real phone call is made: this is the agent's brain, running in your browser.

    What's real here, and what is not
    
    
      
  • Real: a call state machine (greeting, purpose, objections, booking, close), intent detection with global handlers that work at any point (“are you a robot?”, “stop calling me”, “call me later”, “wrong number”, “I want a human”), silence and voicemail handling, a call timer, a persistent call log, and a pre-call compliance check on the recipient's local calling hours and a do-not-call list.
    • 
      
  • Built-in ethics: the agent always says it is an automated assistant at the start and answers honestly if asked. A “stop calling” request puts the number on the do-not-call list and the dial button refuses next time. It never pretends to be human and never calls outside 8 am to 9 pm local time.
    • 
      
  • Not a real phone call, and not an LLM: there is no telephony and no AI model, so the script is rule-based and handles the situations listed above, not open conversation. Real outbound calling also has legal requirements that vary by country (consent, do-not-call registries, disclosure of automated calls); this demo is not legal advice.
