What's real here
  • This is the actual BPE training loop: start with single characters (plus an end-of-word marker), count every adjacent pair across the text, merge the most frequent pair into a new token, repeat.
  • Encoding new text replays the learned merges in the same order. Words the tokenizer has never seen are still handled, just split into smaller pieces.
  • Production tokenizers (GPT, Llama and others) work on bytes and train on terabytes of text, but the core idea is exactly this. Try training on a tiny text: the merges you get depend entirely on the data.
