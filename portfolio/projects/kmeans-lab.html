What's real here
  • This is Lloyd's algorithm: assign every point to its nearest centroid, move each centroid to the mean of its points, repeat until nothing changes. Convergence is detected when no assignment changes.
  • k-means++ seeding picks each new centroid with probability proportional to its squared distance from the existing ones, which usually gives better and more stable results than random seeds.
  • Load "Moons": K-Means draws straight boundaries, so it cannot separate curved shapes. That limitation is the point of that dataset.
