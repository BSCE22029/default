Evolving Rockets

Generation 1 is pure chaos. Each rocket carries a random "DNA" of thrust commands; the ones that get closest breed, mutate, and slowly learn to steer around your obstacles. No physics knowledge is programmed in.

What's real here
  • Each rocket's DNA is an array of 260 thrust vectors, one per frame. Fitness rewards closeness to the target (and speed once it arrives) and punishes crashing.
  • Selection is fitness-proportionate ("roulette wheel", squared to favour the best). Offspring take genes from both parents (single-point crossover); every gene then mutates with the probability you set; the top 5% are always kept unchanged (elitism) and the very best is drawn in amber.
  • Draw a wall right in the way mid-run and watch the population adapt over the next generations. Set mutation to 0% and see evolution stall once diversity is gone.
