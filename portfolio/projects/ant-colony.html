What's real here
  • This is Ant System with an elitist bonus. Each ant builds a tour city by city, choosing the next city with probability proportional to τα · (1/distance)β. After every generation all pheromone evaporates by ρ, then every ant deposits 1/length on its edges and the best tour gets extra.
  • The two baselines are computed on the same cities: a greedy nearest-neighbour tour, and that tour improved by 2-opt local search, a strong classic heuristic. In my test runs on 25 cities the colony beat plain greedy by 10 to 25% and matched or slightly beat 2-opt after 100 generations; results will vary with the map and settings, and 2-opt is a tough opponent. The page tells you which one won each time.
  • Try β = 0 (ants ignore distance) or α = 0 (ants ignore pheromone) to see why both signals matter. None of this is trained or pre-computed; it is all live.
