A working support bot for a fictional café. It answers questions from a knowledge base you can edit, takes orders and table bookings by asking for whatever is missing, and hands over to a human instead of guessing. The panel on the right shows exactly why it answered the way it did.

What's real here, and what is not
  • Real: FAQ answers come from TF-IDF cosine retrieval over the knowledge base (with a light stemmer and a small synonym list), and the bot answers only when the match is strong enough. Orders and bookings use slot filling: it extracts items and quantities (“two lattes and a croissant”), party size, time and day from free text and asks only for what is missing. You can ask a question mid-order and it answers, then returns to the order. Add a Q&A on the right and ask it immediately: it learns instantly.
  • Honest fallback: below the confidence threshold it says it is not sure and offers a human, instead of making something up. “Talk to a human” always works.
  • Not an LLM, and a demo shop: it only knows what is in its knowledge base and menu, so unusual phrasing can miss. The café, prices and address are invented, and orders and bookings are not sent anywhere. To use this for a real business, the knowledge base would be replaced with the business's own content and the order step connected to its system.
