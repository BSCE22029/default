What's real here
  • Training counts how often each word appears in positive and negative examples. Prediction adds up log P(word | class) for every word plus the class prior and picks the larger total: that is Naive Bayes.
  • "Leave-one-out accuracy" retrains the model once per example with that example held out, then tests on it. It is a real estimate of how the model generalises, not a score on training data.
  • Honest limits: it is trained on only about 100 short reviews that I wrote for this demo, so expect roughly 60–70% held-out accuracy and plenty of wrong answers on real-world text: most words in a new review were never seen in training. Add your own examples above and watch the number move. Sarcasm and unseen words defeat it. The value of the demo is seeing every step of the maths.
