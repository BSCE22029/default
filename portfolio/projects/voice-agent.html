Notes
What's real here, and what is not
  • Real: listening uses your browser's built-in speech recognition and speaking uses its speech synthesis (the Web Speech API). The tools are real: the clock uses time zones from a live geocoding lookup, weather comes from the Open-Meteo API, the calculator is a hand-written parser, timers genuinely count down and ring, notes persist in your browser, and Wikipedia answers come from its public API.
  • Privacy, plainly: in Chrome and Edge, speech recognition sends your audio to the browser vendor's cloud service to be transcribed. This page itself records nothing and sends your text only to the public APIs above when a question needs them. If you do not want to use the microphone, type instead. Firefox does not support speech recognition at all.
  • Not an LLM: understanding is rule-based, so it knows the dozen or so things above and says so when it does not understand. I could not test the microphone in my build environment, so I tested the full pipeline by typed input; speech recognition quality depends on your browser and microphone.
