What's real here
  • Tabular Q-learning: after every step the agent updates Q(s,a) ← Q(s,a) + α·(r + γ·max Q(s′,·) − Q(s,a)). The cell colours show max Q, the arrows show the argmax. Nothing is scripted.
  • Rewards: +1 at the goal, −1 in a trap, −0.02 per step (so shorter paths win). Episodes end at the goal, in a trap, or after 80 steps.
  • ε-greedy exploration starts at 1.0 (all random) and decays towards 0.05. Turn on the slippery floor and the agent learns to keep away from traps because a bad slip is now possible.
