    What's real here
    
    
      
  • The tree is grown with CART: at every node it tries every possible threshold on both features, scores each split by weighted Gini impurity, and keeps the best one. Growth stops at the maximum depth, at pure nodes, or when a split would leave fewer samples than the minimum per leaf.
    • 
      
  • Data is split 65/35 into train and test. "Label noise" flips some training labels, which is what makes deep trees memorise mistakes. The curve is recomputed for every depth from 1 to 10 each time.
    • 
      
  • Pruning ideas are visible in the controls: a larger minimum leaf size acts like a brake and often improves test accuracy on noisy data.
