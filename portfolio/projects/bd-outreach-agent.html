What's real here, and what is not
  • Real: the city is looked up live (Open-Meteo geocoding) to get the prospect's country and time zone, and the send times, business-day logic and weekend rules are computed from that. The compliance checker, word counts, follow-up dates and pipeline are real code. Drafts are checked against a do-not-contact list that the triage agent also writes to.
  • Not an LLM, and no web scraping: the writing comes from industry and service templates plus the details you type, so it is only as personalised as your brief. Browsers block reading other companies' websites, so the agent does not research the business itself; the “something you noticed” field is where real research goes.
  • Nothing is sent. “Open in Gmail” and “Open in WhatsApp” only open a pre-filled compose window in your own account, and you press send. I would still send one message at a time to people who plausibly want what you sell.
